RDBMS Technical Highlights
Segment-Wise Seat Allocation

Seats allocated over half-open intervals [origin, dest). Overlap condition: s < r_end AND r_start < e. Allows non-overlapping segment reuse.

3NF Relational Schema

Decouples train catalogs from dated runs, separates passenger profiles from booking transactions, and isolates fare rules from payment logs.

Waitlist & Atomic Promotion

Cancellations trigger atomic re-evaluation of waiting queue entries in strict FIFO order, assigning freed seats within a single transaction.

Concurrency & Integrity

Row-level locking (FOR UPDATE) ensures two simultaneous requests never acquire the same seat segment.
